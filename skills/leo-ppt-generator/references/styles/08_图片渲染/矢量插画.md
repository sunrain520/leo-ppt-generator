# 图片渲染：矢量插画（vector-illustration）

**分类:** 08_图片渲染 · 现代商业

**配对视觉风格:** flat/swiss-minimal 的配图

**定位:** The default safe rendering for general PPT decks. Clean flat vector art with bold shapes, deliberate geometry, and confident negative space. Scales reliably across 15+ slide decks without visual fatigue.

## 1. 风格段落（paste-ready，可直接用于图片生成）

> Clean flat vector illustration with bold geometric shapes and confident solid fills. Crisp outlines (1.5-2px equivalent stroke weight, consistent across all elements) define every form. No gradients within shapes — color is applied as flat blocks. Subtle shadow only where it adds depth (soft 8% opacity drop, no harsh edges). Composition is grid-aware and balanced, with deliberate negative space carrying as much weight as filled areas. Iconography is simplified to essential geometry — recognizable at small sizes. Overall feel is modern, professional, and confidently restrained — a quality often called "design-system aesthetic" in product/SaaS contexts.

## 2. 线条 · 纹理 · 深度

| 维度 | 处理 |
|---|---|
| 线条质量 | Clean vector outlines, uniform stroke weight, sharp corners or consistent rounded radius |
| 纹理 | None — flat fills only. No noise, no paper grain, no painterly artifacts |
| 深度 | Optional 8% soft drop shadow under elevated elements; no perspective rendering |
| 材质 | None — color is information, not material simulation |
| 情绪 | Neutral-positive, professional, designed |

> 色值来自 deck 的 `colors` 锚点，本渲染只描述画法，不写死 HEX；下方 fewshot 中的 HEX 仅为渲染指导。
